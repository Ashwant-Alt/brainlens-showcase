What can AI really tell us about a brain MRI?

This is BrainLens, a research website built to make scan results easier to explore.

Upload a supported MRI slice and review the suggested tumour category.

In this saved example, the dataset label is meningioma. BrainLens also suggests meningioma. That's a match for this image, not proof that every prediction will be correct.

For compatible three-dimensional scans, explore different views, inspect candidate regions, and check their measured volumes.

The core research workflows are built. Experimental two-dimensional tumour outlines still need improvement, so they remain offline. Category suggestions are available separately.

These suggestions aren't confirmed diagnoses. And a negative result doesn't prove a healthy brain.

Our next milestone is independent evaluation using properly separated patient data.

I'm sharing BrainLens to invite feedback and help make the next version better.
