Not sure which image to use?

Use a documented research image whose acquisition information confirms T1 with contrast. If you do not know the sequence or contrast status, do not guess. JPG and PNG are image-file formats; they do not tell us how the MRI was acquired.

Supported images are single-frame JPEG/PNG, 32–4096 pixels per side. T2, FLAIR, WEBP, DICOM, NIfTI volumes, collages and screenshots from the 3D viewer are unsupported here. For compatible NIfTI studies, use the 3D MRI viewer.

What categories has the model learned?

The BRISC research dataset has four image labels: glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumour and no_tumor. The last label does not mean a healthy brain. Acquisition details and patient grouping are limited, and reliable whole-examination detection has not been established.

The installed model has its own saved evaluation records. Published BRISC benchmark results are not a guarantee of BrainLens performance.
